What is placed, not pinned

Four things to pin down.

Every card is filled from something already on the site. Where the month is not pinned the card draws a span rather than a dot, and the span is taken from what the case study already says, never from a guess. Give me a month and the span becomes a dot.

  • The first interviews — Aug–Nov 2025. The span is read from the page: you joined in August 2025, and this is the research the premise was built on.
