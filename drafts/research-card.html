Research card

One shape for every round.

Each card answers the same four questions in the same order, so two rounds can be compared without reading them. Subject, who and how many, when, and what the round settled. A round with four signals and a round with one are the same object.

The anatomy

Four slots, always in this order.

Subject1 — what was tested

0 people ·method month year 2 — who and how many  ·  3 — when

  1. What the round found.

    → What changed because of it.

    4 — signals, one to four of them

The rules

  • The subject is a thing, not a sentence. Explore concepts. The MVP. The mentor flow.
  • The count comes first in the meta line, because it is the number a reader checks.
  • A signal is a finding and its consequence, never a finding alone. If a round changed nothing, the card says so.
  • No slot is left blank. A missing number is marked, not guessed, and the card still reads.

The pivot

6+ people ·one-on-one think-aloud before spring 2026

  1. One-shot AI was not enough. Search understood the intent, then left people on their own.

    → Intent needed a guided path from the ask to the match.

  2. Swiping was not friendly to mentors.

    → The product was rebuilt around the request rather than the person.

    → The stream stayed on the roadmap, behind the mode people already read.

The MVP on TestFlight

9 people ·builds 122–127 June 2026

  1. The AI wrote well, and that was the question.

    → Its brief moved from writing the request to finding what the request is missing.

  2. The words carried more than the design did.

    → A phrasing pass across the product.

  3. Helpers weighed what it would cost them.

    → Filters by deadline and reward before taking something on.
