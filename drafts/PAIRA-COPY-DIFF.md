**Before (79 words)**

> Three constraints shaped the profile. A phone screen is small, so only what helps a decision goes above the fold. **Before (92 words)**

> What that gave us. Experience and education are still there, lower down, and the editor breaks them into small steps so nobody stalls on a blank form. “Open to” switches say what you offer, so nobody asks for what you did not. Points, Request Boost, and settings live here too, because this is where a person decides how much of themselves to put in. The profile is never demanded up front; the app asks for it when it starts to matter, which is what lets onboarding stay short without losing match quality.

**Before (91 words)**

Boost is the piece I care most about: the one paid action that helps the request rather than the ego, so paying feels like moving your ask forward. The purple sheet and the “Request Boosted!” moment are meant to feel like a small celebration, not a checkout.

**Before (88 words)**

> Helpers give the app its supply, and they have less patience than seekers, so the mentor flow had to be the fastest thing in Paira. It opens on suggestion bubbles built from your onboarding intentions and past preferences, sized by how likely each one is to fit, so most helpers reach their matches in two taps. “Tell Us More” is the fuller path: a paragraph in your own words, which the AI reads and turns into tags you can edit before it shows the mentees waiting for you.

**Before (71 words)**

At the end the flow asks one thing, whether to save these answers to your profile, and that is how the mentor flow feeds Explore.
