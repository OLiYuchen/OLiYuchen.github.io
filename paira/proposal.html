What the beta taught us

Signal 1

Browsing people stalled.

Swiping felt familiar, and each card asked for a decision on trust, fit, and value at once. Sessions ended without a next step.

Signal 2

Real requests got answered.

Of the beta’s three features, requests drew the strongest response. A specific need started real conversations on both sides.

Signal 3

One-shot AI was not enough.

Search understood the intent, then left people on their own. Intent needed a guided path from the ask to the match.

These came from 30+ user interviews across both chapters and a round of one-on-one think-aloud tests on the beta, which we designed and ran as a design team: set tasks, potential users, notes shared with the whole team. In February 2026 we began rebuilding the product around the request; the request-first beta shipped in April.
